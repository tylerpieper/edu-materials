# Hardware Recommendations & Research
Checked September 9, 2026. USD list prices from manufacturer pages, before shipping and tax.

**CRITICAL GUIDELINE UPDATE:**
- **Rooftops:** A 1W minimum (30 dBm) is now MANDATORY for all rooftop installations.
- **Handhelds:** The RAK WisMesh Pocket V2 is the top recommendation, strictly paired with a Muziworks 17cm Whip antenna.

## Portable Nodes

- [SenseCAP T1000-E](https://www.seeedstudio.com/SenseCAP-Card-Tracker-T1000-E-for-Meshtastic-p-5913.html): $39.90. GPS in a slim, enclosed radio. Internal antenna and magnetic charging cable. Choose the Meshtastic version.
- [LILYGO T-Echo](https://lilygo.cc/en-us/products/t-echo-meshtastic): From $48.49. E-paper screen, GPS and an 850 mAh battery in a case.
- [RAK WisMesh Pocket V2](https://store.rakwireless.com/products/wismesh-pocket): $89.97. **Top Handheld Recommendation.** GPS, OLED screen and a 3200 mAh battery. Pair with the Muziworks 17cm Whip antenna.

## Boards for DIY Nodes

- [Seeed XIAO nRF52840 kit](https://www.seeedstudio.com/XIAO-nRF52840-Wio-SX1262-Kit-for-Meshtastic-p-6400.html): $13.49. XIAO nRF52840 and Wio-SX1262 radio kit for Meshtastic. Add a case and power source. Check the kit contents before ordering.
- [Heltec Mesh Node T114](https://heltec.org/project/mesh-node-t114/): $17.90 to $33.70. nRF52840 board with optional display, GPS and shell. Choose Rev. 2 or newer. Battery is not in the default packing list.
- [RAK WisBlock Starter Kit](https://store.rakwireless.com/products/wisblock-meshtastic-starter-kit): From $24.99. RAK4631 radio, base board, antennas and USB cable. Add modules as needed. Add a battery and enclosure. Bundles run up to $60.99.

## Solar and Rooftop Nodes (1W REQUIRED)

- **PeakMesh 1W**: High-power 1W (30 dBm) node for rooftop installations. Highly recommended to meet the 1W mandate.
- [RAK Repeater Mini V2](https://store.rakwireless.com/products/wismesh-repeater-mini-v2): $89.90 to $99.90. Assembled solar node with a 3200 mAh battery and IP67 enclosure. You MUST select the 1 W variant for rooftop use.
- [LILYGO T-Deck Plus](https://lilygo.cc/en-us/products/t-deck-plus-meshtastic): From $77.16. Keyboard, color screen, GPS and a 2000 mAh battery. Choose the Meshtastic model.

The hardware slides have been updated to reflect the new 1W minimum mandate for rooftops, highlight the PeakMesh 1W, and emphasize the WisMesh Pocket V2 with a Muziworks 17cm whip antenna for portable usage.
