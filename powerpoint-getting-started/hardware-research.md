# Hardware price research
Checked September 9, 2026. USD list prices from manufacturer pages, before shipping and tax. Starting prices are not confirmed quotes for every US915 variant. Stock and warehouse affect the final price.

## Portable Nodes

- [SenseCAP T1000-E](https://www.seeedstudio.com/SenseCAP-Card-Tracker-T1000-E-for-Meshtastic-p-5913.html): $39.90. GPS in a slim, enclosed radio. Internal antenna and magnetic charging cable. Choose the Meshtastic version.
- [LILYGO T-Echo](https://lilygo.cc/en-us/products/t-echo-meshtastic): From $48.49. E-paper screen, GPS and an 850 mAh battery in a case. Price and stock depend on the frequency and warehouse.
- [RAK WisMesh Pocket V2](https://store.rakwireless.com/products/wismesh-pocket): $89.97. GPS, OLED screen and a 3200 mAh battery. Meshtastic is preinstalled. Includes an antenna and USB cable.

## Boards for DIY Nodes

- [Seeed XIAO nRF52840 kit](https://www.seeedstudio.com/XIAO-nRF52840-Wio-SX1262-Kit-for-Meshtastic-p-6400.html): $13.49. XIAO nRF52840 and Wio-SX1262 radio kit for Meshtastic. Add a case and power source. Check the kit contents before ordering.
- [Heltec Mesh Node T114](https://heltec.org/project/mesh-node-t114/): $17.90 to $33.70. nRF52840 board with optional display, GPS and shell. Choose Rev. 2 or newer. Battery is not in the default packing list.
- [RAK WisBlock Starter Kit](https://store.rakwireless.com/products/wisblock-meshtastic-starter-kit): From $24.99. RAK4631 radio, base board, antennas and USB cable. Add modules as needed. Add a battery and enclosure. Bundles run up to $60.99.

## Solar and Keyboard Nodes

- [RAK Repeater Mini V2](https://store.rakwireless.com/products/wismesh-repeater-mini-v2): $89.90 to $99.90. Assembled solar node with a 3200 mAh battery and IP67 enclosure. 22 dBm and 1 W variants are listed. Check the selected model.
- [SenseCAP Solar P1-Pro](https://www.seeedstudio.com/SenseCAP-Solar-Node-P1-Pro-for-Meshtastic-LoRa-p-6412.html): $100.99. Solar node with GPS and a built-in battery. Select the Meshtastic version. The cheaper P1 leaves out GPS and batteries.
- [LILYGO T-Deck Plus](https://lilygo.cc/en-us/products/t-deck-plus-meshtastic): From $77.16. Keyboard, color screen, GPS and a 2000 mAh battery. Choose the Meshtastic model. Confirm firmware, antenna option and stock.
